    Bio

    I am Chanseok Kang, a Senior AI Software Engineer (Certified AI Specialist) at LG Electronics AI Lab (CTO Division) since 2014. Building on an early foundation in embedded systems, OS power management, and device drivers, I bring reinforcement learning (RL) control into mass‑produced physical systems—most notably shipping the world’s first commercial offline RL spin‑cycle controller in LG washing machines.

    My current research and engineering focus on qualifying and deploying quantized neural policies across edge accelerators (Jetson Orin, Hailo‑8, Coral Edge TPU, Qualcomm QNN) and building fault‑isolated Physical AI & robotic systems (SO‑101 manipulation, QNX 8.0 RTOS).

    • On-Device Neural Control & Physical AI Audits: Hardware‑in‑the‑loop paired task audits for quantized controllers (NeurIPS 2026 ODI), process‑isolated real‑time robot control on QNX 8.0, and multimodal rollout inspection tooling.
    • Commercial Appliance RL Deployment: Continual offline RL for washing‑machine spin vibration suppression (PCT Patent First Inventor), video‑labeled sensor reward learning for household dryers (IEEE CASE 2026), adaptive refrigerator compressor control, and RL‑tuned HVAC PID control.
    • Academic & Community Contributions: Conference author and reviewer (IEEE CASE), Korean translator of Grokking Deep Reinforcement Learning (2022 NAS Excellent Academic Book), and technical reviewer for 35+ AI and systems books.
